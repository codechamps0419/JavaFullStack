# AI Learning Guide

First, focus on understanding **what each concept is, why it is used, and one simple application example**.  
Do not try to learn every topic deeply during the first reading.

With around five years of Java development experience, most interviews will mainly check whether you understand common AI terms, their purpose, and how they connect to backend applications.

They usually will not expect deep AI or mathematical knowledge unless the role is specifically AI-focused.

Once the basic concepts are clear, continue learning the remaining advanced topics from this guide whenever you have time.

The goal is to build practical awareness first and gradually deepen your knowledge.
